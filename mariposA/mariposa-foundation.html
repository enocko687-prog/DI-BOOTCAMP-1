ABOUT MARIPOSA FOUNDATION

Our Mission, Vision & Values

We are committed to restoring hope and creating opportunities for street-connected children, orphans, and vulnerable young people to build stable and meaningful lives.

🎯

Our Mission

To restore hope and create pathways for street-connected children and orphans through education, mentorship, spiritual guidance, livelihood support, and practical care that helps them live with dignity.

👁️

Our Vision

A society where every child, regardless of background, has the opportunity to grow into a confident, productive, and fulfilled adult who can contribute meaningfully to their community.

❤️

Our Core Values

  • Love & Compassion
  • Integrity & Transparency
  • Empowerment
  • Faith, Hope and Spiritual Discipline
  • Community & Unity

WHAT WE DO

Our Programs

📚

Education Support

Providing school supplies, uniforms, books, and learning materials so vulnerable children can stay in school and build a better future.

🍲

Food & Basic Needs

Providing nutritious meals, basic shelter, clothing, and essential care to children living in hardship and uncertainty.

🤝

Mentorship & Counseling

Offering mentorship, emotional support, life-skills training, and guidance that helps young people grow into responsible and resilient adults.

✝️

Spiritual Guidance

Sharing hope and faith while helping young people grow spiritually and discover their purpose.

⚽

Talents & Recreation

Nurturing talents through sports, creativity, skills development, and livelihood-focused activities that help children thrive.
