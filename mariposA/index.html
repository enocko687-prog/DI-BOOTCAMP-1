A few helpful details

Good questions, welcome.

Still wondering if we’re the right fit? Here are a few things people often ask before getting in touch.

Ask us something else →
What kind of businesses do you work with?+

We partner with founders, growing teams, and established businesses who care about how their brand shows up. We’re especially helpful when you’re launching something new, repositioning, or ready to improve a digital experience.

Do I need to know exactly what I need?+

No. You can start with the challenge you’re facing or the outcome you want. We’ll ask questions, understand the context, and suggest a focused first step before preparing a proposal.

Can you work with our existing team or developer?+

Yes. We can collaborate with your internal team and provide organized design files, documentation, and a handoff that supports implementation. Development scope should be confirmed in your proposal.

How long does a project take?+

It depends on the scope, decision-making, and the number of deliverables. After our first conversation, we’ll outline milestones and a realistic schedule so everyone knows what happens next.

How do pricing and proposals work?+

We tailor the scope to your goals and priorities. Before work begins, you’ll receive a written proposal that sets out the deliverables, timeline, investment, and any assumptions.

What happens after I send an inquiry?+

We’ll review the details and arrange a first conversation to learn more, answer questions, and see whether the project is a good fit. There’s no obligation to proceed.
