SESSION PREPARATION

Materials for meaningful mentoring.

Use this practical checklist to prepare welcoming, useful and well-organized sessions. Adjust it for participants' ages, activities and access needs.

01

Session essentials

  • Session plan and goal for the meeting
  • Attendance sheet and pens
  • Notebooks, pencils and name tags
  • Quiet, accessible meeting space
02

Learning and activities

  • Whiteboard or flip chart with markers
  • Age-appropriate books and worksheets
  • Art, reflection or problem-solving supplies
  • Games or equipment for planned activities
03

Safety and wellbeing

  • Program code of conduct and safety contacts
  • Required consent and emergency details
  • First-aid kit and drinking water
  • Relevant support and referral contacts
04

Reflection and follow-up

  • Goal-setting and reflection sheets
  • Action points for the next session
  • Participant feedback forms
  • Secure place for approved program records

Privacy reminder: Collect only the information your program needs and store participant records securely, following your organization's safeguarding and consent procedures.
